FigBasic


Six figures are included to FigBasic.

All figures can change color through the Attributes Menu, and the Caption can also change font, size and style.

		
Line
# of anchorpoints: 2 or more
Expandable
Appearance: Displays a line, or arrow, between the anchorpoints. 
	

	
Activate: Opens a dialog for choosing line type (line, single arrows, double arrow), and thickness.

Rectangle
# of anchorpoints: 2
Appearance: Displays a rectangle with the anchorpoints as corners. 
	

	
Activate: Opens a dialog for choosing the thickness, or to fill it.

Polygon
# of anchorpoints: 3 or more
Expandable, cyclic
Appearance: Displays a polygon with the anchorpoints as corners.
	

	
Activate: Opens a dialog for choosing the thickness, or to fill it.
	
Circle
# of anchorpoints: 2
Appearance: Displays a circle, the first selected point as its center and the second selected point defining its radius.
						


Activate: Opens a dialog for choosing the thickness, or to fill it.

Caption
# of anchorpoints: 1
Appearance: Displays a text string.


	
Activate: Opens a dialog for changing the displayed text and the position of the anchorpoint.

Oval
# of anchorpoints: 2
Appearance: Displays an oval, with the two anchorpoints defining its bounding rectangle.
	

	
Activate: Opens a dialog for choosing the thickness, or to fill it.
	
ClosedBezier
# of anchorpoints: n, where n >= 3 AND n MOD 3 = 0
Appearance: Displays a closed Bezier curve as defined by the Ports module.



Activate: Opens a dialog for choosing the thickness, or to fill it.

OpenBezier
# of anchorpoints: n, where n >= 4 AND n MOD 3 = 1
Appearance: Displays an open Bezier curve as defined by the Ports module. Can also be an arrow, just like Lines.



Activate: Opens a dialog for choosing curve type (curve, single arrows, double arrow), and thickness.





ComputedPoints

Six computed points are included. The figures present in the pictures below are drawn just for illustrating purposes, they don't belong to the point.
	

Cornerpoint
# of anchorpoints: 2
A new point is created in one of the corners of the box defined by the anchorpoints. The new point is in one of the corners without an anchorpoint. The point can be moved to the opposite corner. 



Rectanglepoint
# of anchorpoints: 2
A new is created on the rectangle defined by the anchorpoints. The point can be moved on the rectangle. 



Circlepoint
# of anchorpoints: 2
A new point is created the circle defined by the anchorpoints. The point can be moved on the circle.



Projection
# of anchorpoints: 3
The first two anchorpoints define a line that the third anchorpoint project down to for the position of the computed point.



Center Point
# of anchorpoints: 2
The computed point is in the middle of the anchor points.
