FigPoints


Six computed points are included.
The figures present in the pictures below are drawn just for illustrating purposes, they don't belong to the point.
	

Cornerpoint
# of anchorpoints: 2
A new point is created in one of the corners of the box defined by the anchorpoints. The new point is in one of the corners without an anchorpoint. The point can be moved to the opposite corner. 



Rectanglepoint
# of anchorpoints: 2
A new is created on the rectangle defined by the anchorpoints. The point can be moved on the rectangle. 



Circlepoint
# of anchorpoints: 2
A new point is created the circle defined by the anchorpoints. The point can be moved on the circle.



Projection
# of anchorpoints: 3
The first two anchorpoints define a line that the third anchorpoint project down to for the position of the computed point.



Center Point
# of anchorpoints: 2
The computed point is in the middle of the anchor points.
